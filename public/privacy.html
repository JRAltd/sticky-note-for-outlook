Privacy Policy — Email Sticky Notes

Effective Date: October 2026

Email Sticky Notes (including the Google Workspace Add-on for Gmail, the Chrome/Edge Browser Extension for Gmail, and the Microsoft Outlook Add-in) is built with a strict privacy-first architecture. Your email content and sticky notes never leave your own Google Account, Microsoft 365 mailbox, or local browser storage.

1. Where Your Notes Are Stored

  • Microsoft Outlook Add-in: Notes are stored directly inside your own Microsoft Exchange mailbox via Office.js CustomProperties and RoamingSettings (and optional Microsoft Graph Open Extensions com.jraltd.emailNotes when enabled).
  • Google Workspace Add-on for Gmail: Notes are stored exclusively in your own private Google Account via Google Apps Script PropertiesService.getUserProperties().
  • Chrome / Edge Browser Extension for Gmail: Notes are stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local and localStorage.

2. Zero Third-Party Data Collection or Transmission

Email Sticky Notes does not operate any external database, analytics tracker, advertising network, or third-party server. We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share your emails, message metadata, sticky notes, or personal information with any third party.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use & AI Disclosures)

Google Workspace / Gmail Limited Use Compliance:
Email Sticky Notes' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, the Google Workspace Add-on requests only the minimum required scopes:

  • gmail.addons.current.message.metadata: Used solely to read the active message's ID, subject line, and sender name/address while the message is open so sticky notes can be linked to that specific email. The add-on never reads or accesses the body of your emails.
  • script.storage: Used solely to save your sticky notes privately inside your own Google Account's UserProperties store.
  • gmail.addons.execute: Used to render the Sticky Notes user interface cards inside the Gmail companion sidebar.

Prohibition on AI/ML Model Training: Data obtained through Google Workspace APIs is never used to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) and/or machine learning (ML) models.

4. Data Protection Mechanisms and Security Safeguards

Security & Data Protection Statement:
We implement rigorous technical and organizational safeguards to ensure user data and Google user data are protected against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

Encryption in Transit

All communications between your browser, the add-on client interface, and Google APIs are encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols, including Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 or higher) and secure HTTPS connections. Unencrypted connections are never permitted.

Encryption at Rest

User data and sticky notes are stored exclusively within Google's secure cloud infrastructure (via Google Apps Script PropertiesService.getUserProperties()) and/or in local browser storage protected by your operating system's built-in file encryption. All Google infrastructure employs robust AES-256 encryption at rest. No user data is ever stored on unencrypted storage media or external servers.

Access Controls & Human Access

Strict access controls prevent unauthorized access. Because notes are stored directly within the end-user's private Google account or local device, JRAltd developers and personnel have no access to, cannot view, and cannot extract your emails, metadata, or notes. No human access to Google user data is granted or technically possible.

5. Permissions Used (Outlook & Chrome Extension)

  • Outlook Add-in: Uses mailbox permissions solely to read the active message subject/sender context, save your sticky notes on the message, and apply the visual 📝 Sticky Note category tag in your inbox.
  • Chrome Extension for Gmail: Uses storage, sidePanel, and *://mail.google.com/* host permission solely to render the sticky notes sidebar and top-of-email summary banner inside Gmail and save your notes locally in Chrome.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Because all sticky notes are stored inside your own account or local browser storage, you retain full ownership and control over your data at all times:

  • Retention: Notes persist only for as long as you choose to keep them associated with your emails or account.
  • User Deletion: You can permanently delete individual notes or purge all stored notes at any time with a single click using the in-app delete and clear functions.
  • Revoking Access: You can revoke the application's access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account Third-Party Connections page and removing Email Sticky Notes. Revoking access immediately prevents the add-on from accessing your account.
